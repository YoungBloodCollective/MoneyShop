## Full Description

MoneyShop is the digital platform that simplifies your access to the best loan offers in Romania.

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Calculate - Enter your income and desired loan type
2. Verify - We analyze your eligibility 100% online (tax authority + credit bureau)
3. Compare - Receive personalized offers from 10+ partner banks
4. Apply - Submit your application directly from the app

WHAT WE OFFER:
- Loan simulator for personal loans and mortgages
- Advanced calculator with FICO score and credit bureau analysis
- Offer comparison from BCR, BRD, ING, Banca Transilvania, UniCredit and more
- Fast and secure identity verification (KYC)
- AI assistant chat for financial questions
- Free consultation scheduling with authorized broker
- Real-time application status tracking

BENEFITS:
- 100% FREE - no fees for brokerage services
- FAST - preliminary analysis in minutes
- SECURE - encrypted data, GDPR compliant
- AUTHORIZED - registered credit broker (ANPC)

LOAN TYPES:
- Personal loans
- Mortgage loans
- Refinancing
- Joint application (combined income)

MoneyShop is not a credit institution and does not grant loans. The platform facilitates intermediation between users and partner financial institutions. Final credit conditions are determined by partner banks.
